# FilmLab

A RAW converter with film emulation for the Mac. Open a CR3, ARW, NEF, RAF or DNG, pick a film look (warm and cool colour film, slide, cinema and black-and-white) rendered on your own photo, fine-tune it, and export a JPEG or TIFF. Everything happens on your own Mac: your photos are never uploaded.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click the **.dmg** file.
2. Drag **FilmLab** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. It's signed by Tungl Media and approved by Apple, so it opens straight away (macOS just asks once whether you want to open an app you downloaded).

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a card pops up in the middle of FilmLab. Click **Update Now** and it downloads, checks the update's security signature, and reopens by itself. You can also choose **FilmLab ▸ Check for Updates…** any time.

FilmLab only ever connects to this page, to look for updates. It sends nothing about you or your photos.

## Privacy

FilmLab collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. It only goes online to look for updates on this page, and that check sends nothing about you, your Mac or your photos. GitHub, which hosts this page and the downloads, sees those requests like any web visit ([GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).
