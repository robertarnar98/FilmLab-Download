# FilmLab

A RAW converter with film emulation for the Mac. Open a CR3, ARW, NEF, RAF or DNG, pick a film look (Kodak, Fujifilm, Leica, Cinestill, Ilford…) rendered on your own photo, fine-tune it, and export a JPEG or TIFF. Everything happens on your own Mac: your photos are never uploaded.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the newest version](https://github.com/robertarnar98/FilmLab-Download/releases/latest)**: click the **FilmLab-….dmg** file under *Assets*.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click the **.dmg** file.
2. Drag **FilmLab** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app: click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a card pops up in the middle of FilmLab. Click **Update Now** and it downloads, checks the update's security signature, and reopens by itself. You can also choose **FilmLab ▸ Check for Updates…** any time.

FilmLab only ever connects to this page, to look for updates. It sends nothing about you or your photos.

Needs macOS 13 Ventura or newer · Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer) · by Tungl Media
